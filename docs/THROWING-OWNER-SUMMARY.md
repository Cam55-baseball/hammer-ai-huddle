# Throwing cards — where they stand (for the owner)

**Short version:** the throwing cards are prototypes. Nothing on them should be trusted until we run a real throwing clip through them.

## Works today (in testing)
- **Spotting the move.** The card can tell a sideways shuffle apart from a forward move like a crow hop or walk-through, and it refuses anything it can't tell.
- **Energy angle** only turns on for a sideways throw from a shuffle, and switches itself off for a crow hop or walk-through — as you ruled.
- **Early shoulder opening** and **head staying still** reuse the pitching measurements, which only look at the body up to release.
- On a still clip and on swing clips, every throwing measurement correctly says "nothing to measure."

## Needs redefining for field throws
- **Tempo.** On the mound it runs from peak leg lift to front-foot landing. A fielder has no leg lift, so we need your call on where the gather starts in a crow hop or shuffle.
- **Separation (hips vs shoulders)** and **stride.** Both lean on mound landmarks — the rubber, a set position, a stride toward the plate. On a field throw we need to agree what the start point and target line are.
- **Lift & Thrust does not transfer.** As written it is about a pitcher's leg lift and push off the rubber. A crow hop loads differently, so it needs redesigning for crow-hop loading, not a relabel.

## What we need from you
1. A real throwing clip or two (side-on, whole body in frame) — a crow hop, and a shuffle if you can.
2. Where the gather starts for tempo on a field throw.
3. Whether you want a crow-hop version of Lift & Thrust, and what "good" looks like.
