- No drill sets an arm slot, release point or "correct" shape. The athlete moves the way their body moves.
- Stop rule: sharp pain, pinching, or pain that changes how you move → stop and tell a coach or parent. This is training, not treatment.
- Video slot: empty — owner records.

## A. PITCHER CONDITIONING — prime the springs before an outing
Session type: `cond.pitcher_conditioning` (day before start, reliever available days). Goal: leave springy, not tired. Whole session 8–12 minutes.

### PC1. Tall Pre-Tension Hold (split stance) — OWNER (order), PROPOSED (shape)
- **Set-up:** Stand in a short split stance, glove-side foot forward. Arms reach overhead like you're grabbing a bar.
- **Steps:** 1) Press the floor away with both feet. 2) Reach up and slightly back, long through the front of your body. 3) Breathe slowly and hold.
- **Cue:** "Long and loaded."
- **Right feels like:** a stretch-tension line from your front foot up through your stomach to your fingers.
- **Wrong feels like:** low-back pinch, holding your breath.
- **Common mistake:** arching the low back instead of getting long.
- **Dose:** 2 × 20 seconds each stance.

### PC2. Ankle Pogos — OWNER (elastic tier U1), SOURCED (Flanagan & Comyns 2008)
- **Set-up:** Feet hip-width, knees soft, standing tall.
- **Steps:** 1) Bounce off the balls of your feet. 2) Keep the ground touches short and quiet. 3) Stay tall, arms relaxed.
- **Cue:** "Hot floor."
- **Right feels like:** a bouncy, springy rhythm; calves and feet doing the work.
- **Wrong feels like:** heavy landings, heels crashing, knees bending a lot.
- **Common mistake:** jumping high instead of bouncing quick.
- **Dose:** 2 × 15 contacts. Stop when they stop being quiet.

### PC3. Skip-to-Stick Crossover — PROPOSED
- **Set-up:** 10 yards of space.
- **Steps:** 1) Skip forward with an easy arm swing. 2) Every fourth skip, land on one foot and hold for a count of two. 3) Switch the stick foot each time.
- **Cue:** "Bounce, bounce, bounce, stick."
- **Right feels like:** rhythm in, solid balance out.
- **Wrong feels like:** wobbling on the stick, stomping.
- **Common mistake:** going fast. This is rhythm, not speed.
- **Dose:** 2 × 10 yards.

### PC4. Rotational Rebound Throw (light med ball, sub-max) — OWNER (plyo-ball wall series family 17, sub-max), PROPOSED (dose)
- **Set-up:** Side-on to a solid wall, 2–3 metres away, light med ball (1–2 kg) at your back hip.
- **Steps:** 1) Shift into your back hip. 2) Turn and toss the ball into the wall at about 60% effort. 3) Catch it on the bounce and flow straight into the next one.
- **Cue:** "Catch it, use it."
- **Right feels like:** the ball's bounce loading you for the next throw; the whole body turning together.
- **Wrong feels like:** arms doing all the work; straining.
- **Common mistake:** throwing hard. The day before a start, it should feel easy.
- **Dose:** 2 × 6 each side. Never on start day.

### PC5. Build-Up Strides ×3 — SOURCED (Rhea et al. 2008), PROPOSED (dose)
- **Set-up:** 30 yards, flat ground.
- **Steps:** 1) Start jogging. 2) Build smoothly to about 80% by the middle. 3) Float out the last third.
- **Cue:** "Smooth gets fast."
- **Right feels like:** relaxed shoulders, quick feet, ready.
- **Wrong feels like:** straining, out of breath.
- **Common mistake:** turning it into a race. Walk back fully between each.
- **Dose:** 3 strides, full walk-back.

**Left out (no owner doctrine or source covers it):** any timed conditioning test for pitchers; band/cable "arm priming" volume prescriptions; any fingertip or grip loading (blocked day before by addendum §C3).

## B. RECOVERY CONDITIONING — flush, easy, circulatory
Session types: `cond.pitcher_conditioning` day-after-start variant, recovery flush on sore days / after heavy load. Goal: move blood, restore easy motion, add no stress. Whole session 10–15 minutes. Talk-test easy the whole time.

### RC1. Easy Bike or Walk — SOURCED (Dupuy et al. 2018)
- **Set-up:** Bike, or a flat walk outside.
- **Steps:** 1) Go at a pace where you can talk in full sentences. 2) Keep it steady. 3) Breathe through your nose if you can.
- **Cue:** "Easy enough to chat."
- **Right feels like:** warm, loose, a bit better than when you started.
- **Wrong feels like:** breathing hard, sweating heavily.
- **Common mistake:** turning recovery into a workout.
- **Dose:** 8–10 minutes.

### RC2. Long Reach Walk — OWNER (whole-chain), PROPOSED (shape)
- **Set-up:** 10 yards of space.
- **Steps:** 1) Take a slow walking step. 2) Reach the opposite arm up and long as you step. 3) Feel the stretch run from the back foot to the reaching hand. Switch sides each step.
- **Cue:** "Long from foot to fingers."
- **Right feels like:** a gentle full-body stretch, no strain.
- **Wrong feels like:** forcing the range, pinching in the shoulder.
- **Common mistake:** going into pain to "get it loose." Stay in easy range.
- **Dose:** 2 × 10 yards.

### RC3. Easy Rhythmic Bounce (no jump) — OWNER (elastic, rhythm), PROPOSED (dose)
- **Set-up:** Feet hip-width.
- **Steps:** 1) Gently bounce through the knees and ankles without leaving the floor. 2) Let your arms swing loose. 3) Keep it soft and rhythmic.
- **Cue:** "Shake it out."
- **Right feels like:** loose, light, no effort.
- **Wrong feels like:** jumping, tension in the shoulders.
- **Common mistake:** turning it into pogos. Feet stay down.
- **Dose:** 2 × 30 seconds.

### RC4. Slow Breathing Reset — PROPOSED
- **Set-up:** Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat.
- **Steps:** 1) Breathe in through your nose for 4. 2) Breathe out slowly for 6. 3) Let your ribs drop on every breath out.
- **Cue:** "Long breath out."
- **Right feels like:** calm, heavier on the floor.
- **Wrong feels like:** dizzy (slow down) or forcing it.
- **Common mistake:** rushing.
- **Dose:** 2 minutes.

**Left out:** foam rolling / soft-tissue tool dosing, cold or contrast water, compression, sleep protocols — no owner doctrine recorded and these drift toward treatment claims. Listed for owner decision.
