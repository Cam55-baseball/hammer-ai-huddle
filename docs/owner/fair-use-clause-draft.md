**Coach and Organization plans — fair use.**
1. Each Coach account, and each Organization coach seat, is for one named person. Logins may not be shared.
2. Plan features, including analyses, may be used only for players the account holder coaches. They may not be resold, sublicensed, or offered as a paid service to third parties.
3. Analyses are unlimited in number but subject to an automatic speed limit (about 30 per account per hour) to protect the service. Requests above the limit are paused, not charged.
4. We may contact you, pause access, or end the plan if use clearly breaks these rules. Before ending a plan, we will give notice when reasonably possible.
