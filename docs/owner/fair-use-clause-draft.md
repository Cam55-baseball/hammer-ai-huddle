**Coach and Organization plans — fair use.**

(4) If an account breaks these rules, we may suspend or end the plan immediately. Where reasonable, we will warn you first.
(5) Plans ended for breaking these rules are not refunded for unused time, consistent with our Subscription Policy. If we end a plan by mistake, we will restore it or refund the affected charge.
