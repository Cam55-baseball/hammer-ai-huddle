# Hammers Modality — Scouting, Recruiting & Facility Plan, version 3.1 (final for approval)

October 11, 2026 · Includes every owner decision so far. Built only after you approve, and after the security fixes now underway.

## 1. Security first (in progress)

- Videos and scout files become private. Only allowed viewers can play them, and old public links stop working.
- People can no longer give themselves roles, join organizations on their own, approve their own follows, or fake access.
- The rules for minors become the same on every screen.

## 2. Consent: one time, up front, easy to change

**Teens 13–17.** At sign-up, on the same screen as the injury waiver, the parent sets:
- scouting visibility
- coaching connections
- health-data sharing with connected coaches (its own checkbox, as the law requires)
- academics sharing

After that, the teen accepts connections themselves. The parent gets a notice for each new connection.

**Under 13.** The parent runs the player's account, with no separate parent account. Scouting is allowed when the parent turns it on. Scouts contact the parent through the app. (Your lawyer must OK the updated wording first.)

**Scouting visibility switch.** It sits in Profile → Settings near the top: "Scouting visibility: On / Off". Families are told about it at sign-up, in "Before you start," and in the privacy pages. When it's on, scouts see everything the parent (or a player 13+) chooses to share, from one clear checklist:
- name and photo
- school
- town or region
- grad year
- academics
- performance numbers
- game stats and game video
- reports
- grades
- parent contact

Health data has its own separate checkbox.

## 3. Accounts, organizations and teams

**At sign-up:** "I'm a player, a coach, or an organization (facility, team, school, academy)." Organizations are created right there and go into your existing approval queue, with an official-email code check. Approved organizations get a "Verified" badge.

**Organization vs. team:**
- An organization is the verified business or program. It has admins, coaches and sponsorship billing.
- A team is a group inside it (14U, Varsity, Pitchers), with its own roster and coaches.

**A coach with no organization:**
1. Taps Coach Dashboard → "Create team" and enters a name, sport and age group or level.
2. The app quietly creates a small organization in the coach's name.
3. The coach invites players by code, link, QR code or request.
4. The coach can get verified later, to connect with minors, or move the team into a facility.

**One request system for everything:** organization ↔ coach, coach ↔ player, organization ↔ player, and scout → player. Whoever receives a request taps ✓ Accept or ✗ Deny. Everything lives in one "Requests" inbox.

**Facility staff** see only the athletes assigned to them.

**Head coach:**
- One per player, chosen from the coaches they're connected to, directly or through an organization.
- Gets first alerts and leads the player's guidance. Other coaches are assistants or position coaches.
- Never gets extra data beyond what the player or parent allowed.

**Payments:**
- Athletes pay their own subscription, or an organization sponsors them.
- If sponsorship ends, the player gets a grace period and can continue on their own payment, keeping all their history.

## 4. Coach tools

- **Coach Dashboard:** a clean roster, the Requests inbox and teams.
- **Coach View for each athlete:**
  - A quick summary card: today's status, last activity, 7-day completion, top alert, newest personal best or test, and head coach.
  - Detailed tabs: logs, trends against the player's own baseline, tests, body load and pitch counts, game stats and video, reports, grades, the six-week recap, readiness and pain (with consent), and private notes.
- **Messages:**
  - Coaches message connected players directly.
  - For players 13–17, the parent is automatically copied.
  - For players under 13, messages go to the parent.
  - All messages are saved, and any message can be reported.
- **Send workouts and practices:**
  - Coaches build them from your Exercise Vault, or schedule a practice, for one player or a whole team.
  - It appears on the player's plan as a "From Coach" card.
  - The safety rules check it first: rest days, pitch counts, age and phase. If it breaks a rule, the coach sees why and gets a safe alternative.
  - Players log it like any other card. Practices count toward workload, so the next plan adjusts.

## 5. Organization tools

**Facility dashboard.** One short line per player: the name plus the single most important message, such as "Missed 2 days," "New PB: 10-yd 1.52," "Test due Friday" or "All good ✓." Tapping the name opens:
- today's status
- last activity
- 7-day completion
- tests due
- alerts
- what changed

Filters by team or coach.

**Compare athletes in the organization:**
- 2–6 athletes side by side, or a team view: level, positions, tests, personal bests and trends, game stats, completion and grades.
- Each number shows its date and how it was verified.
- It warns when a comparison isn't fair (different position, age or level).

**Growth and game-performance trends** (new). This shows how in-app training lines up with game results over time, for each player and each team. It brings together:
- training: completion, strength, speed and velocity trends
- **video analysis reports:** mechanics scores and findings from each report, using only what the report actually measured, with report dates and counts
- game stats

For example, a player's swing-analysis scores and bat speed rising alongside their batting results, or a pitcher's delivery-analysis scores next to their walks and strikeouts.

The rules for these trends:
- Shown only when there's enough data, with the number of games and tests and the time period.
- Clearly labeled "these move together." The app never claims training caused a game result.
- Says "not enough data yet" when that's true.

## 6. Scout tools

- **Scout Dashboard:** watchlist, search (sport, position, grad year or age group, level, region, bats/throws, verified numbers, recent tests, open to recruiting) and follow requests.
- **Scout Profile for each athlete, in tabs:** Overview, ID and academics, Performance, Game play and stats (new), Reports and video, Six-week recap, Grades (your 20–80 scale) and Players Club.
- **Game play and stats.** Game logs and box-score stats:
  - hitting: at-bats, hits, extra-base hits, walks, strikeouts, stolen bases
  - pitching: innings, hits, runs, walks, strikeouts, pitch count, velocity
  - fielding: putouts, assists, errors
  - game video clips
  - pitch-location and batted-ball charts, only from real tap-in data
  Each stat is labeled athlete-entered or coach/organization-verified.
- **Compare athletes:** 2–4 side by side, with the same fairness warnings, showing only what each family shared.

## 7. Onboarding

- **Positions:** primary and secondary.
- **Hands:** bats/throws and pitching hand.
- **Playing level:** level and team, grad year, region, and "open to recruiting."
- **Optional academics:** high school or college GPA, SAT, ACT composite and ACT writing. Labeled athlete-reported, with an optional transcript upload the school or facility can verify.
- **Removed:** pitch mix.

## 8. The General page, two clean layers

- **Player view:** progress against the player's own starting point, personal bests, tests, the six-week recap and season position.
- **Scout overview:** who they are, strengths and areas to develop with evidence and sources, game stats, grades, the recap, and report counts.

Missing data shows "Not yet measured." Nothing is made up.

**Proof at every step:**

## Owner additions (Oct 12)

OWNER ADDITIONS TO PLAN v3.1 (coach and organization tools, Phases 5–6). APPROVED; build them in those phases. Same rules: no publish, no cron changes, no iOS project changes, never deploy with unexplained errors, never fabricate, prove everything, clean up test data, tick off roadmap.md.

THE OWNER'S MISSION: Hammers is each player's 1-on-1 coach, and the human coach uses this view to check whether Hammers is truly developing the player. Everything must be real, transferable coaching information.

1. A "DEVELOPMENT" TAB in each athlete's Coach View (also visible to org admins for rostered athletes):
   a. ANALYSIS SUMMARY BY CATEGORY (hitting, pitching, throwing, fielding, baserunning; whichever exist): the MOST RECENT 5 reports in each category, showing:
      - key scores with a trend arrow
      - recurring issues and whether each is being CLEANED UP (e.g. "lead-leg block flagged in 4 of the last 5 → 1 of the last 5")
      - new issues
      - a plain one-paragraph summary
      Use only what the reports actually measured.
   b. STRENGTHS AND WEAKNESSES: a clear, evidence-based list drawn from tests, analysis reports, game stats and training logs. Each item shows its evidence and date, and updates as new data comes in.
   c. CONNECTIONS (inferences). Link analysis reports, game stats (including the pitch-location and batted-ball heat maps from real tap-in data), tests, and training and practice trends.
      - Example: a swing-mechanics score rising alongside a rise in hard contact to the pull side in games; a delivery fix alongside a drop in walks.
      - Label each one by evidence level: Observed fact / Measured change / Supported pattern / Hypothesis to check / Not enough data.
      - Show the counts and time window for each. Never claim one thing caused another.
   d. COACHING TAKEAWAYS: 1–3 actionable, transferable focus points, each with its evidence and confidence (e.g. "Focus next: lead-leg block. It shows in 3 of the last 5 swing reports, and game chase rate is up over the last 4 games.").
   e. DEVELOPMENT REPORT CARD: what Hammers prescribed, how much was done, and what changed since joining (tests, report scores, game stats), with dates. This is how the coach judges Hammers' development of the player.
2. STRENGTHS AND WEAKNESSES are also visible on the Coach Dashboard roster (on tap) and in the organization comparison.
3. TESTING DAY FOR FACILITY ATHLETES:
   - When a rostered athlete's phase test is coming up, notify the assigned coach and the head coach 3 days ahead and again on the day, with the test list.
   - The coach can schedule the session, run it, and enter or verify results in the app, tapping "Verified in person" with an optional video link. Those results are labeled facility-verified.
   - If the athlete tests alone, the results are labeled athlete-entered.
   - Reschedule and skip options. All tests follow the existing safety rules (readiness, pain, age, phase).
4. COMPARE BY TEST RESULTS: coaches and org admins can compare and rank their athletes by test results within the organization or team. Filters: age group, position, level, verified only. Show dates and verification, with fair-comparison warnings.
5. PROVE IT with test accounts:
   - a player with 5+ reports per category, game stats and tests, whose Development tab shows real summaries, the cleaning-up status, strengths and weaknesses, labeled connections and takeaways
   - a player with too little data showing "not enough data"
   - testing-day notifications arriving at the coach, and a verified result
   - comparison by test with filters
   - access limited to assigned coaches and org admins
   - phone screenshots
