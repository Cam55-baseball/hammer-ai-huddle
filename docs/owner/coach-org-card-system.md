# Coach and organization card system

## Where to start

On the Coach Dashboard, open **Send workouts**. On the Organization dashboard, open **Workouts → Send workouts**. Both dashboards have the same **How it works** panel, with pictures of the buttons.

You can work only with players whose training you are allowed to open. Organization assignments and the player's sharing choices still apply. Joining an organization does not open private health details.

## 1. Send a workout

1. Pick players or a team in Send workouts.
2. Choose Build workout. Find exercises in the Exercise Vault or skill drills, build with your own items, or use Saved templates.
3. Check every exercise, amount of work, rest, equipment and note.
4. Choose One day, Picked dates, Repeating, or Any day — player picks.
5. Choose Preview player card. Review what the player will see, including locked fields and choices.
6. Choose Send workout. Read any safety warning. Okay continues only when the checks allow it; Cancel leaves the work unchanged.

## 2. What the player sees

The named notice tells the player who sent the workout and its scheduled day. On that day, the offer appears at the top of Today under **From Coach**. It is also available in **My Custom Activities → Coach workouts**.

- **Use instead:** use the workout in place of matching planned work, after the safety check.
- **Add alongside:** add the workout to the day, after the safety check.
- **Save for later:** keep the offer on the Coach workouts shelf without claiming any work was done.

Staff use Send workouts and Sent workouts, not the player inbox. A notice or preview is not proof the player received, accepted or completed work; check the saved response and results.

## 3. Allow a skip

1. Open the player's profile → Player week.
2. Pick the current or upcoming day and expand a card.
3. Choose Allow skip on the card or a single activity.
4. Add a reason if helpful, then Okay.
5. The player sees the coach's note and a Skip (allowed) button. The player chooses whether to skip.

The permission alone is not a completed activity. If the player chooses the allowed skip, that item is excused without lowering Discipline.

## 4. Remove work for this session

Use the same day details. Choose **Remove for this session**, add a note and confirm. The item or card is removed for that dated session only. It is excused from Discipline, but it is not recorded as performed work. Other dates, preparation and entered results remain protected. Player week and Sent workouts keep who made the decision, when, what changed and the reason.

## 5. Edit a day and save a template

1. On an editable card in Player week, choose Edit this day.
2. Change exercises, their order, work amounts, rest, intensity and notes.
3. Choose Save this day. Read warnings before Okay or Cancel. Under-18 edits require a typed reason and parent notice.
4. When saving succeeds, the day's replacement names the coach in **Because: edited by Coach [name]** and notifies the player.
5. To keep the card for reuse, enter a Template name and choose Save as template. Saving a template does not itself edit a day.
6. In Send workouts → Saved templates, choose Apply to…, choose a player and day, then Apply to this day. Safety is checked again for that player and date.
7. Or choose Send workouts on the template to schedule and send it as an offer.

Not every card can be edited. Preparation, already-logged work, unclear replacement targets and stale season or growth information can block an edit. Do not interpret a refusal as a save.

## 6. Read Player week and Sent workouts

The calendar shows Monday through Sunday. Pick a day to expand its cards, activities and actual entered results. Use the arrows for another week and This week to return. These views read existing workout logs and task records; they do not create completion counters or rebuild plans.

### Calendar statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Done ✓ | All required activities have recorded completion; excused activities do not prevent completion. This is not a skill grade. |
| Partly done | Some work is recorded, but required work remains unfinished or was cut short. |
| Not done | No recorded completion for required work. It does not prove the player made no effort. |
| Skipped (allowed) | The player used an allowed skip, or the whole card consists of excused items. Not performed work. |
| Removed by coach | The coach removed or replaced the work for that session. Historical results and decisions stay visible. |
| Rest day | A saved rest-day plan; recovery or other saved tasks may still appear. |
| No saved plan | No readable saved plan for that date. Not a zero score or a completion failure. |

### Sent workout responses

| Response | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Not yet answered | The offer has no saved player response. |
| Used instead of their card | The matching day work was replaced through the checked save. It still needs completion. |
| Added alongside | The checked workout was added. It still needs completion. |
| Saved for later | The offer is kept for later; no completion is claimed. |
| Declined | The player declined the offer. |
| Cancelled | The send was cancelled; this is separate from the player's response. |
| Done: X of Y activities | Recorded completion of the activities created by the accepted workout. Read the exercise results below it. |

## What counts toward Discipline

Discipline uses existing required-work completion records, not how many offers were sent or templates saved. Accepted work is recorded through the normal workout and task logs. An allowed skip that the player actually chooses and a mandatory session removal are excused; neither lowers Discipline or fabricates reps, sets or measurements. Permission to skip without using it does not itself finish work. Saving, previewing, receiving or declining an offer is not recorded exercise completion. Unexcused unfinished required work remains unfinished. Discipline is not a judgment of character or ability.

## Safety rules that still apply

- The same access, sport, equipment, readiness, load and injury checks apply to sends, choices, day edits and template applications.
- Some warnings can be acknowledged with Okay; blocks cannot. Cancel must not save changes.
- Under-18 edit warnings require a typed reason and parent notice. A coach's explanation does not remove a safety block.
- Already-entered results, preparation and other dates remain intact. A dated edit or removal is not a permanent program change.
- Fresh information is required. If the saved day or season facts changed, refresh and retry; do not bypass the refusal.
- Hammers' own generator season restrictions remain unchanged. An allowed coach warning is not permission to weaken the generator.
- Shared training access does not grant private measurements or health details without the required consent.
