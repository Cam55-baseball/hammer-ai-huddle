# Coach game and practice logging — design proposal (NOT built)

Plan 4 §D keeps logging by coaches OFF until the right player is always tagged. Proposal for the owner:

## Option 1 — One player at a time (recommended first)
- The coach opens a connected player's page and taps "Log for this player". The player's name and photo stay pinned at the top of every screen.
- Each saved entry is labeled "coach-entered, [coach name], [date]" and sent to the player to confirm or flag. Flagged entries are excluded from ratings until resolved.
- Wrong-player risk: very low (one player per session, a visible lock).

## Option 2 — Lineup mode (later)
- The coach picks a saved lineup. Every play needs a tap on the player's jersey number plus a name check before saving.
- Undo for 10 seconds; the player gets a confirm/flag request the same as in Option 1.
- Only after Option 1 has run cleanly with real teams.

## Rules for both
- A connection is required; under-13 entries follow the parent consent rules.
- Never overwrite player-entered stats; show both with sources.
- Every number carries a date and source.

Owner decision needed: approve Option 1 to build, Option 2 later, or neither.
