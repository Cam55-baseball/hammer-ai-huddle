# Legal v2 drafts — scouting consent (Plan v3.1 Phase 2)

DRAFT for lawyer review. NOT saved as a new version, NOT shown to anyone. Approved v1 texts stay unchanged.
The app will not switch to these until the owner confirms the lawyer approved them.

## 1. Child Safety & Communication Policy (child-safety v1 → v2), section "What minors see"

BEFORE (v1):
> - Under-13 accounts are parent-controlled: no public profile, no scout or coach visibility, and no contact without the parent.
> - Players 13–17 control recruiting visibility only with a parent's yes, item by item (profile, metrics, video, contact).

AFTER (v2 draft):
> - Under-13 accounts are parent-controlled and never public. A parent may choose to let approved scouts and coaches see items the parent picks (profile, metrics, video, health, academics). Scouts and coaches can never contact a child under 13; any contact goes only to the parent.
> - Players 13–17 control scouting visibility only with a parent's yes, item by item (profile, metrics, video, health, academics, contact). Health and academics are always separate choices and start off.

## 2. Privacy Policy (privacy v1 → v2), section 5 "Children (COPPA)", second bullet

BEFORE (v1):
> - We collect from a child only what the training features need. Children cannot make their profile public, cannot be contacted by scouts or coaches without the parent's permission, and cannot message other users outside approved channels.

AFTER (v2 draft):
> - We collect from a child only what the training features need. Children cannot make their profile public and cannot message other users outside approved channels. A parent may choose to let approved scouts and coaches see specific items (profile, metrics, video, health, academics) and can turn this off at any time, which ends access immediately. Scouts and coaches can never contact a child under 13; they may contact only the parent.

## 3. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (consumer-health-data v1 → v2), section 5 "Who receives it"

BEFORE (v1):
> With your separate yes: coaches, scouts or partners you choose.

AFTER (v2 draft):
> With your separate yes: coaches, scouts or partners you choose. Sharing health information with scouts or coaches is its own switch, separate from every other sharing choice, and starts off. For a minor, only a parent can turn it on.
