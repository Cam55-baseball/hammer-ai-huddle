# Archived features

## Enrollment demo purchase interception — October 25, 2026

The colorful Unlock Now button previously bubbled to the subscription tile's navigation. On `/pricing` or `/checkout`, `DemoGate` redirected accounts with pending demo progress to `/start-here`. This enrollment walkthrough is different from the regular Demo button's spotlight tour.

Purchase pages and plan selection no longer trigger that redirect. Unlock Now explicitly opens plan choice with the selected sport and tier. The walkthrough remains available for existing non-purchase training onboarding; it therefore has a practical place and was not archived or deleted. All demo records and the regular Demo button remain untouched.
