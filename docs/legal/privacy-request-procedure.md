# Privacy Request Procedure — DRAFT for attorney review

## How people ask
- In the app: Settings → Legal & privacy → Download my data / Delete my account / consent switches (behind legal_v2).
- Email: hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org.
Every request is saved in `privacy_requests` with a due date (30 days by default) and shows in the owner queue at /owner/legal-records.

## Steps
1. **Verify identity.** In-app requests are verified by the sign-in. Email requests: reply from the account's email; for a child, verify the parent who signed the Parent Promise.
2. **Download my data:** the app builds a file from every table the account owns (same list as account deletion) and the user's consent records. Done instantly.
3. **Delete my account:** in-app deletion runs immediately (`delete-account`). Consent records are kept 3 years (marked account ended), as the Retention Policy says.
4. **Withdraw consent:** health-data and sharing switches save a "withdrawn" record at once; collection or sharing stops.
5. **Correct:** fix in the profile, or owner edits on request.
6. **Close** the request in the queue with a note. Deadlines: 30 days (Washington MHMDA allows 45; we use the shorter); denied requests get a reason and appeal path (appeal by email, answered within 45 days).
