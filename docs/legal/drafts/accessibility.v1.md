# Accessibility Statement (v1, DRAFT)

Hammers Modality wants every player, parent and coach to be able to use the app.

# Our goal
We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Today we: support screen readers with labels on buttons and controls; keep text contrast at AA levels; make tap targets at least 44 pixels; respect "reduce motion" settings; and let pages zoom and reflow on phones.

# Known limits
Some video-analysis overlays, charts and older pages may not yet be fully accessible. We are working on them.

# Tell us
If something is hard to use, email [hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org](mailto:hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org) or use **Report a problem**. We aim to reply within 5 business days and can help another way while we fix it.
