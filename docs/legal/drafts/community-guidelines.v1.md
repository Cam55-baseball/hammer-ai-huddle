# Community & Content Guidelines (v1, DRAFT)

These rules apply to everything you upload, write or share in Hammers Modality.

# Allowed
Training videos, game footage you have the right to share, honest feedback, respectful questions.

# Not allowed
- Nudity or sexual content, and any sexual content involving a minor (reported to authorities)
- Harassment, bullying, hate, threats or encouraging self-harm
- Videos of other people without their permission (and a parent's permission for minors)
- Content you do not own or have rights to (see [Copyright (DMCA) Policy](/legal/copyright))
- Spam, ads, scams, or sharing someone's private information
- Dangerous challenges, or encouraging training through pain or injury
- Impersonating someone, or lying about stats, age or credentials

# What we do
We may remove content, limit features, suspend or close accounts. Tell us if you think we got it wrong at hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org.

# Reporting
Use **Report a problem** or email [hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org](mailto:hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org).
