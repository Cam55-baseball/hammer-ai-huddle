# Child Safety & Communication Policy (v1, DRAFT)

Many Hammers Modality players are minors. Keeping them safe comes before everything else, including recruiting, sales and engagement.

# Rules for every adult on the platform (coaches, scouts, trainers, parents)
- No private one-on-one contact with a minor through the app without the parent or guardian's permission. Recruiting contact with a minor requires the parent's authorization.
- Keep communication about training, in approved app channels, and visible to the parent on request.
- Never ask a minor for personal photos, personal contact details, money, or to meet in person through the app.
- No sexual, violent, harassing, bullying or discriminatory messages or content.

# What minors see
- Under-13 accounts are parent-controlled: no public profile, no scout or coach visibility, and no contact without the parent.
- Players 13–17 control recruiting visibility only with a parent's yes, item by item (profile, metrics, video, contact).

# Reporting
Use **Report a problem** in the app or email [hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org](mailto:hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org). Reports about a child's safety are reviewed first. We may suspend accounts immediately, preserve evidence, and report suspected child exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (CyberTipline) and law enforcement. In an emergency call 911.
