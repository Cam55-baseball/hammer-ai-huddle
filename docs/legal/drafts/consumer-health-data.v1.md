# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy (v1, DRAFT)

This policy explains how **Hammers Modality LLC** handles "consumer health data" as defined by the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and similar laws in other states (for example Nevada and Connecticut). It adds to our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

# 1. Consumer health data we collect
- Sleep, readiness and recovery answers
- Pain, soreness, injury history, return-to-play status and illness
- Nutrition, hydration and food photos
- Height, weight, limb measurements and body goals
- Body-movement measurements taken from your training videos
- Optional mental-performance and mood journal entries
- Data that could suggest health status, such as arm-care or workload limits

# 2. Where it comes from
From you (or your parent), from the videos you upload, and from our software's analysis of what you log.

# 3. Why we collect and use it
To build and adjust safe training plans, apply safety rules (for example lower work when pain or poor readiness is reported), show progress and report cards, and answer your questions. We use it only for these purposes.

# 4. Your consent
We collect and use consumer health data only after you (or your parent) say **yes** in the app. We **share** it with a third party (other than our service providers) only after a **separate yes** — for example before connecting a partner such as PitchLab. You can withdraw either consent anytime in **Settings → Legal & privacy**.

# 5. Who receives it
Service providers that process it for us: Lovable Cloud (hosting), Google Gemini and OpenAI (analysis), Resend (only if a safety email includes it). With your separate yes: coaches, scouts or partners you choose. We **never sell** consumer health data. [LAWYER: confirm the "sale" analysis and whether AI providers are processors.]

# 6. Your rights
You may confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data; get a list of every third party and affiliate that received it with contact details; delete it; and withdraw consent. Use Settings → Legal & privacy or email [hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org](mailto:hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org). We answer within 45 days. You can appeal a denial by replying; we answer appeals within 45 days, and you may contact the Washington Attorney General.

# 7. Geofencing
We do not set up geofences around health care facilities.
