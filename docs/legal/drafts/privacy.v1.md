# Privacy Policy (v1, DRAFT)

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information **Hammers Modality LLC** (15985 Preserve Marketplace #1154, Odessa, FL 33556; [hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org](mailto:hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org)) collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it, and your rights. Health-type information is also covered by our [Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy](/legal/consumer-health-data).

# 1. What we collect
- **Account and profile:** name, email, password (stored hashed), date of birth, sport, positions, throwing and batting side, state, team, graduation year, profile photo, role (player, parent, coach, scout).
- **Training and performance:** prescribed and completed work, logs, pitch counts, speeds, times, strength numbers, game stats, standards and report cards.
- **Videos and body-movement analysis:** videos you upload and measurements our software takes from them (body positions, angles, timing, speeds, ball tracking).
- **Health-type information:** sleep, readiness, soreness and pain, injuries and recovery status, illness, nutrition and hydration logs, food photos, body measurements (height, weight, limb lengths), body goals, and optional mental-performance journal entries.
- **Payments:** plan, billing status and Stripe customer ID. Card numbers go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them.
- **Messages and support:** help-chat and AI-coach conversations, problem reports, emails you send us.
- **Device and usage:** device type, app version, screen size, pages used, errors, IP address (for security and consent records), and notification settings.
- **Location (approximate):** a ZIP code or city if you enter one, used for weather.

# 2. Why we use it
To run your account; build and adjust your training plans; analyze your videos and movement; keep training safe (for example pitch counts, pain and readiness rules); process payments; send account and safety emails; answer support; protect the service; meet legal duties; and, only with permission, share with people you choose (parents, coaches, scouts). We do **not** sell personal information and do **not** use it for targeted advertising.

# 3. Who receives it (service providers)
- **Lovable Cloud** (built on Supabase) — hosting, database, file storage, sign-in.
- **Google (Gemini AI)** — main AI provider for video and movement analysis, plans, food-photo reading and chat.
- **OpenAI** — backup AI provider for the same tasks if Google is unavailable.
- **Stripe** — payments and subscriptions.
- **Resend** — sending account, receipt, parent and support emails.
- **Roboflow** — ball-tracking for pitch-velocity measurement (staff-only feature today).
- **Weather services** (U.S. National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, Zippopotam.us, OpenStreetMap Nominatim) — receive only a ZIP code or place name.
- **PitchLab** — only if you connect it and give a separate yes; not connected today.
- **Apple** — if you use the iPhone app or Apple purchases.

# 4. Anonymous training data
To improve our programs we keep a de-identified training store: prescriptions, completions and measurement numbers with names, emails, birthdates, photos and videos removed, ages and dates rounded into bands, and a scrambled ID that cannot be reversed without a secret key. It never leaves Hammers Modality and is never sold or used to train other companies' AI. You (or a parent for a child) can turn this off anytime.

# 5. Children (COPPA)
- Children under 13 can only use Hammers Modality through a parent-controlled account. A parent or guardian must read our Parent Notice, sign, and complete a card payment before the child's account works. [LAWYER: confirm this is a valid verifiable-parental-consent method.]
- We collect from a child only what the training features need. Children cannot make their profile public, cannot be contacted by scouts or coaches without the parent's permission, and cannot message other users outside approved channels.
- Parents can review their child's information, download it, correct it, stop further collection, withdraw permission, and delete the child's account at any time from the parent account or by emailing us. After permission is withdrawn we stop collection and delete the child's data within 30 days, except records the law requires us to keep.
- If we learn we collected a child's information without parental permission, we delete it.

# 6. How long we keep it
- Account, profile and training data: while the account is open, then deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
- Videos: while the account is open (or until you delete them), then within 30 days.
- Health-type data: same as account data; deleted within 30 days of account deletion or withdrawal of consent.
- AI chat and help-chat logs: 12 months.
- Problem reports and support emails: 2 years.
- Payment and tax records: 7 years (legal requirement).
- Consent and agreement records (including waivers): at least 3 years after the account ends.
- Security logs: 12 months.
- De-identified training data: kept, because it no longer identifies anyone.

# 7. Your rights and how to use them
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, download, correct and delete your information, to withdraw consent, and to appeal our decision. In the app go to **Settings → Legal & privacy** to download your data, delete your account, or turn off consents. You can also email hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org. We answer within 30 days (45 days where the law allows, with notice). We do not discriminate against you for using your rights. If we deny a request you can appeal by replying to our answer; if still unhappy, you can contact your state attorney general.

# 8. Security
We use encryption in transit, access controls, role-based permissions and limited staff access. No system is perfect; if a breach affects you we will notify you as the law requires.

# 9. Changes and contact
We will post updates here and tell you about important changes. Questions: hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org, Hammers Modality LLC, 15985 Preserve Marketplace #1154, Odessa, FL 33556. These policies are governed by Florida law except where your state's privacy law applies.
