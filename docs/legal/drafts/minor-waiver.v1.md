# The activity
Baseball and softball training prescribed or shown by the Hammers Modality app, including throwing, pitching, hitting, sprinting, base running, strength training, jumping, med-ball and weighted-ball work, and conditioning, done at home, at a field or at a gym, without Hammers Modality staff present.

# Inherent risks
Inherent risks are the dangers or conditions, known or unknown, that are characteristic of, intrinsic to, or an integral part of the activity and that are not eliminated even if Hammers Modality LLC acts with due care in a reasonably prudent manner. They include: arm, shoulder and elbow injuries; muscle strains and joint sprains; being struck by a ball or bat; falls; heat illness; concussion; the failure to warn of an inherent risk; and the risk that my child or another participant may act in a negligent or intentional manner and contribute to my child's injury or death.

# Waiver and release
As my child's natural guardian, I waive and release, in advance, on behalf of my child and myself, any claim or cause of action against Hammers Modality LLC and its owners, affiliates, employees and agents that would accrue to my child for personal injury, including death, and property damage resulting from an inherent risk in the activity, as allowed by section 744.301(3), Florida Statutes. This waiver does not release claims beyond what that statute allows.
