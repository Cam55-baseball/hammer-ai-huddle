# Medical & Safety Disclaimer (v1, DRAFT)

**Hammers Modality is a training tool, not a doctor.** Nothing in the app — plans, drills, analyses, report cards, AI answers or readiness scores — is medical advice, a diagnosis or treatment.

# Before you train
- Get a doctor's OK before starting, especially after an injury, illness, surgery, or if you have a heart, breathing or other medical condition.
- A parent or guardian should supervise players under 18. Train with qualified adult supervision and proper equipment.
- Warm up fully. Follow the app's rest days, pitch counts and throw limits.

# Stop right away and get help if you have
- Pain in your arm, elbow, shoulder, back or anywhere else that does not go away
- Chest pain, fainting, dizziness, trouble breathing or a racing heart
- Numbness, tingling, or a head injury
Call 911 in an emergency.

# Limits of the software
Plans and video analysis are created by software, including artificial intelligence. They can be wrong, incomplete or not right for your body. Camera angle, lighting and video quality affect results. Use your judgment and listen to your coach, athletic trainer and doctor over the app.

# Risk
Throwing, hitting, sprinting, lifting, jumping, med-ball and weighted-ball work, and conditioning can cause injury, including serious injury or death. You choose whether and how hard to train.
