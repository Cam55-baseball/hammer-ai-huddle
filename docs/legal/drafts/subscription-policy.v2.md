This policy is written to meet California's Automatic Renewal Law (Bus. & Prof. Code §17600 et seq.) and similar laws in other states and federal rules.

# 1. How plans renew
Paid plans **renew automatically** at the end of each billing period (monthly, every 6 months or yearly, as shown at checkout) at the price shown, until you cancel. Before you pay, we show the price, the renewal period, that it renews automatically, and how to cancel, and you check a separate box to agree. We email you a confirmation with these terms and how to cancel.

# 2. Free trials and promotions
If a plan starts with a free trial or discount, we show when it ends and the price you will pay after. You will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

# 3. Reminders and price changes
- Yearly plans: we email a reminder **15 to 45 days** before each renewal with the price and how to cancel.
- Price changes: we email you at least **30 days** before a new price applies, and you can cancel before it does.

# 4. How to cancel — online, in a few taps
Go to **Settings → Legal & privacy → Cancel subscription** and tap **Cancel**, then confirm. No phone call or extra steps. You can also email [hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org](mailto:hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org). If you bought through Apple, cancel in your Apple ID subscriptions. We email a cancellation confirmation and keep a record.

# 5. What happens when you cancel
Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, then stops renewing. Your data stays in your account unless you delete it.

# 6. Refunds
- We do not give partial refunds for unused time, except where the law requires.
- We do not offer refunds based on athletic progress or results.
- If you believe you were charged after cancellation or charged in error, email hammersmodality@hammersmodality.org so we can review the charge. This does not limit any refund or other rights required by law.
- Apple purchases are refunded through Apple.
