# Reply to App Review — Hammers Modality 1.0 (4)

Hello, and thank you for the review. We have addressed all three items in build 1.0 (4).

**Guideline 4 – Design (sign-in in the default browser)**
Sign-in and registration now happen entirely inside the app. Email and password sign-in, sign-up, password reset and parent sign-up use the app's own screens. Sign in with Apple opens in an in-app browser sheet and returns the user to the app signed in; the Safari app is never opened. The app offers no other third-party sign-in.

**Guideline 2.3.6 – Age rating (In-App Controls)**
The app asks every user for a birthdate (age assurance). Players under 13 can only use the app through a parent-controlled account set up by a parent or guardian. That account has Parent controls: view the child's data, download or delete it, withdraw permission, and turn training-data sharing on or off.

To see them on iPad, sign in with the under-13 demo account in App Review Information, then:
1. Tap **Menu** (top left) and choose **Profile**, or tap the round profile picture (top right) and choose **Profile**.
2. Scroll to **Parent controls**.

To delete an account (any account):
1. Tap the round profile picture (top right) → **Settings · Delete account**, or scroll to the bottom of **Profile** → **Delete account**.
2. Type DELETE and confirm. The account is deleted and the user is signed out.

**Guideline 2.1(b) – Business model**
1. Who uses the paid content: athletes 13 and older, parents of athletes under 13, and coaches who subscribe to a training plan.
2. Where they buy it: only on our website, hammersmodality.org, through Stripe. On the United States storefront the app shows a "Subscribe on our website" button that opens our website for the same signed-in account (Guidelines 3.1.1(a) and 3.1.3). The app is available in the United States only, and on any other or unknown storefront it shows no prices, buttons or links to buy.
3. What previously purchased content they can access: plans bought on the website (Complete Pitcher, 5Tool Player, Golden 2Way) unlock their daily training plans, video analysis, report cards and training modules after sign-in.
4. What is unlocked without a purchase: the account, profile, settings, Parent controls, account deletion and the app's free areas. Nothing is sold through In-App Purchase.

Both demo accounts in App Review Information have full access, so no purchase is needed during review.
