# App Review Notes — paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes

Two demo accounts are listed in App Review Information (the sign-in details are entered there):
- Adult demo player: full subscription access, ready to use. Sign in with email and password.
- Under-13 demo child: a parent-controlled account (approved by a test parent). Use it to see Parent controls: Menu (top left) → Profile → scroll to "Parent controls".
Delete account (any account): profile picture (top right) → "Settings · Delete account", or the bottom of Profile → "Delete account".

Purchases: Hammers Modality is a multiplatform training service. Subscriptions are sold only on our website, hammersmodality.org, through Stripe; nothing is sold through In-App Purchase. On the United States storefront only (checked with StoreKit Storefront), the app shows a "Subscribe on our website" button that opens our website checkout for the same signed-in account, as Guidelines 3.1.1(a) and 3.1.3 allow for US storefront apps. The app is available in the United States only; on any other or unknown storefront it shows no prices, purchase buttons, links or instructions. People who bought on the website sign in and use what they paid for.

Sign-in: everything happens inside the app. Sign in with Apple opens in an in-app sheet and returns to the app.
