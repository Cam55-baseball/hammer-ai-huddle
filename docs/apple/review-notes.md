# App Review Notes — paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes

Two demo accounts are listed in App Review Information (the sign-in details are entered there):
- Adult demo player: full subscription access, ready to use. Sign in with email and password.
- Under-13 demo child: a parent-controlled account (approved by a test parent). Use it to see Parent controls: Menu (top left) → Profile → scroll to "Parent controls".
Delete account (any account): profile picture (top right) → "Settings · Delete account", or the bottom of Profile → "Delete account".

Purchases: Hammers Modality is a multiplatform training service sold only on the United States App Store. Subscriptions are sold only on our website, hammersmodality.org, through Stripe; nothing is sold or paid for inside the app and there is no In-App Purchase. As Guidelines 3.1.1(a) and 3.1.3 allow for US storefront apps, the app shows a "Subscribe on our website" button that opens our website in Safari. If StoreKit reports a storefront other than the United States, the app hides all prices, buttons and links.

Where to find the "Subscribe on our website" button:
1. Sign-in screen (before signing in): scroll below "Landing Page" and tap **Subscribe on our website** — Safari opens hammersmodality.org/pricing.
2. Signed in as the adult demo player: on the **Training Dashboard**, scroll to the plan tiles (Complete Pitcher, 5Tool Player, Golden 2Way). On any plan that isn't unlocked, tap **Subscribe on our website** — Safari opens our website signed into the same account.
3. Pricing: tap the title area of a locked plan tile on the Training Dashboard: each plan shows **Subscribe on our website**.
4. Coach plans: at the bottom of **Pricing**, tap the coach plans link (or the coach plan tiles at the bottom of the Training Dashboard) to open **Staff Plans**; each plan shows **Subscribe on our website**.
People who bought on the website sign in and use what they paid for.

Sign-in: everything happens inside the app. Sign in with Apple opens in an in-app sheet and returns to the app.
