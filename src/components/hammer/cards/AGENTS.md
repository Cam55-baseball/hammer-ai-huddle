# Hammers Today cards
- Work timers share ExerciseTimer; pause/reset/restart do not author results, and stop/completion reports active elapsed hundredths through existing logs. Why: targets and paused time are not performed work.
- Run rest uses `_shared/wic/runRest.ts`; PAP keeps lift-to-action rest separate from after-sprint rest, and conditioning counters accept declared base-route reps only, sharing session state through `ConditioningRestProvider` across log portals. Why: changing exercise pop-ups must not reset bases or rest, change lifting or grant new running work.

- Today card grouping is a presentation-only partition preserving original drill objects and completion keys; popup details use a shared context. Why: grouping must not author training or duplicate logging.
- Bunting partitions keep the hitting modality and original task/Swap identities, omit combined templates/duration, and register distinct presentation card IDs. Why: a separate bunt card must neither duplicate swings nor claim the full hitting block.
- Owner video references use the existing library player ahead of slug-based Vault lookup. Why: a drill's saved guidance must show its actual attachment, including external platform videos.
- Hammers Today order, 'Next up', day ring and stage story come only from `todayRhythm.tsx`, fed by prescribed cards' progress; full-day presentation also requires saved morning/night and applicable pre-lift checks. Why: presentation must never author training or call skipped checks a completed day.
- Release presentation supplies no athlete-facing availability countdown or midnight promise. Why: the owner removed release-time wording; preparation time is not delivery time.
- Activity pop-ups host each exercise’s entry grid and its timers via one shared log portal; exercise disclosures retain outcomes, surveys and specialized extra logging without duplicating entry state or timers in parent panels. Why: logs remain visible while information stays closed and per-exercise saving stays canonical.
- Power-primer round recording and rest timers use the same pop-up log host, while its warm-up lock and safety controls remain in its disclosure. Why: relocating controls must not create new lift sets, bypass safety or change record-only saving.
- Guided PAP continuations bind to the canonical lift prescription ID and row; the cursor preserves lift-to-action and sprint recovery as distinct intervals. Why: the primer must run between lift rows, not after the entire lift, without a second prescription.
- Input-confirmation plan cards remain visible separately from prescribedToday; reminders, counts and completion still require ready drill work. Why: an equipment gate must be actionable without becoming a training prescription.
- Embedded practice messages are same-origin, frame-checked refetch hints; only matching self/sport/module/token canonical session readback supplies entered set counts. Why: video or a child window must never manufacture completion or bypass readiness.
- Guided cursors persist exercise identity and own their rest clock; editable standalone timers cannot change guided advancement. Why: remounts and timer controls must not move the player to another exercise or shorten saved rest.
- Unknown-duration guided rest persists its pending state; Finished flushes canonical entries before declared rest starts. Why: reloads and additional navigation buttons must not replay a save or bypass recovery.
- Guided rest hides the mounted work surface without unmounting canonical rows; one timer reset/restart control changes with timer state. Why: recovery is a distinct page and removing duplicate controls must preserve drafts and active elapsed time.
- Cut-short presentation belongs to a known-target early stopped timer without a manual value; partial numeric rows say Work recorded without changing canonical completion outcomes. Why: UI wording must not recalculate performed work or mislabel typed entries.
- Component timers reset by canonical row identity and use only that row's manual-time flag. Why: an earlier circuit component must not supply elapsed time or suppress another component's timer outcome.
