# Prescription rules
- Restricted defense sustains its purchased floor only with explicit dry siblings of canonical position drills; leg restrictions use seated reads with no arm or leg loading. Why: normal fielding ceilings must not erase compatible non-loading skill work.
- Pitching cards partition existing arm work through pitchingDay and guardArmDrills before schedule/stage reductions. Why: card identity never authorizes a second pitch dose or arm budget.
- Final skill ordering uses only complete declared prep/drills/full-go/log stages and never moves owner slots or changes doses. Why: game-speed work must follow preparation without inferred intensity or new training.
- Beginner defense guidance cannot remove required protective gear or add a competing receiving dose. Why: a tier note must not contradict the legal card's setup and volume.
- Catcher framing declares four sets, one per zone, in the existing sets/reps syntax so the existing tier multiplier applies only to reps. Why: spatial-zone wording must not bypass beginner dose scaling.
- Recovery exercise rows contain only timed mobility and breathing with their own setup/stop guidance; hydration, wake-time and injury-note reminders never become exercise results. Why: untracked advice must not create fake performed work or unrelated how-to text.
- Recovery card/template minutes equal the retained timed rows, without an undeclared reminder allowance. Why: removed reminders must not leave unexplained time in the day's prescription.
- Recovery guides defer to the row's prescribed timer and carry its explicit stop guidance in inline overrides. Why: expanded how-to must neither change duration nor lose a safety stop.
- Whole-day rest uses shared explicit softRecovery identities at final-block and final-save boundaries, distinct from lift-spacing removals; recovery warmups bypass training anchors. Why: mobility labels and defense overrides cannot certify training as rest.
- Bunting uses sourced evidence and active Vault catalog identities inside existing legal hitting slots; attempts replace swings one-for-one, owner/prep/full-go slots remain protected, whole off-season eligibility requires all four permitted quarters. Why: bunting may not create days, borrow unknown traits, bypass phase eligibility or add volume.
- Catalog alternative equipment requires every mandatory item and one complete alternative group; normalize ownership and missing answers identically, and reject malformed gear arrays. Why: OR setups must neither require both options nor silently drop required gear.
- Name-only legacy athlete adjustments never replace or remove prescribed drills; canonical universal candidates require explicit intent, current availability and safety facts, then fresh atomic server certification before writing. Why: a copied dose or invented alternative name is not proof of equivalent legal work.
- Warm-up ORDER comes only from `warmupStages.ts` (stage → order-within-stage → builder order, applied after choosing); it never adds/removes movements, and hard rules (tissue prep first, max intent after build-ups) override Vault stage tags. Why: owner 2026-10-11 — choose the old way, order cold to ready.
- Practice load is projected from exact logged category/sport/date with unknown minutes/intensity preserved; schedules never stand in for actual work. Why: category isolation and honest workload require source records, not estimates.
- Practice-dose adjustments require structured sets and the row's explicit floor; missing floors leave volume unchanged, and no skill is reopened or global workload-high set. Why: minimums, lifting and earlier safety decisions outrank reductions.
- Bounded practice-dose targets use whole sets near 70% of the current legal dose, never below half or the explicit row minimum, with reps unchanged; only post-safety ready skill rows may call it. Why: the owner reduction must preserve full-go availability and never undo freshness or lifting protections.
- Cross-reference (mixedSignals) only informs one category's priority/Because and never authors work; other categories' signals are ignored. Why: no category spill-over.
- Mixed-signal video direction requires explicit boolean findings on a non-future fresh recording; missing/empty measurements are not clean. Why: missing evidence or explicit false findings must not fabricate a conflict or improvement.
- Mixed-signal hitting game direction requires the existing game-plan sample floor from actual AB/pitch counts, including clean percentages; absent/invalid counts emit no hitting game direction. Why: an empty or thin ledger cannot fabricate disagreement with a video.
- Actual practice reaches ready skill cards through practicePlanContext after safety, with source IDs, entered load and missingness; streaks add Because and missing minima keep doses unchanged. Why: auditable context is not a guessed reduction or learning outcome.
- Fielding execution uses explicit catalog non-throwing read metadata, never name-pattern eligibility; prep/owner slots and existing stop rules remain protected. Why: a fielding stage cannot grant new throwing work.
- Running execution likewise requires catalog scenario identity, not matching names; doses and first/owner slots stay fixed. Why: renamed or analysis-selected drills cannot grant game-speed permission.
- Practice source reads reject caps/missing data; conflicting same-ID records contribute no load in either order. Why: incomplete or ambiguous history cannot invent weekly totals or streaks.
- Extra weekly goal blocks count included scheduled lifting and speed days as well as ladder skill days against availability, and use only an existing phase-template slot. Why: skill-only counts understate the athlete's full training week.
- Judged mastery ranks feed the existing weekly goal opportunity only for unsaved days, preserving baseline, subscription, phase and availability checks; no second boost. Why: evidence should choose the existing priority, not manufacture more work.
- Unknown-equipment hitting retains dose slots and chooses best available work from planningGear assumptions with explicit stop rules. Why: unknown ownership is not an athlete's missing-equipment answer.
- The daily-plan caller supplies current subscription modules to the weekly goal boost through priorityAccess, intersected with ready modalities; explicit empty/opposite-sport modules grant no purchased skill boost. Omitted modules preserve generic builder behavior. Why: a ready baseline card is not proof of a purchased goal category.
- Generated equipment uses planningGear assumptions minus explicit Always missing identities; fielding keeps required-use safety stops and same-category dry siblings for unavailable gear. Why: unknown inventory cannot suppress a purchased floor, and missing gear cannot authorize unsafe catching.
- Unknown-position fielding also stays a non-prescribing confirmation card, even with gear; never add generic accuracy throws as a fallback. Why: position-specific catalog and arm-work rules cannot authorize an unknown-role throw dose.
- Pitching execution stages originate in EASS catalog metadata and survive the actual daily-plan mapping; sore-arm exclusion reads that metadata, never a name. Why: a presentation stage must preserve the existing legal pitch count and cannot authorize extra throws.
- Fully declared preparation circuits retain component measures and set counts in the existing task log; mixed/unequal components get individual rows and untouched targets remain unperformed. Why: a circuit cannot copy a neighbor's dose or count prefills as performed throws.
- Compound preparation needs an explicit entry for every component before guided advancement; declared rest wins and rep-only circuits otherwise use warm-up recovery. Why: recording one movement must not skip its neighbors or strand preparation on an unspecified rest screen.
- Skill days come from the top (sustain) ladder for everyone with Sunday (SIX_DAY_REST_DOW) as the only whole rest day; readiness, never week or training age, sets low-taxing intensity; only youth-band and injury clamps lower days. Why: owner Oct 28 6-day week with no ramp.
- Scheduled Sunday games never reopen training at final composition, and pitching analysis entitlement retains pitcher defense. Why: schedule posture and module aliases cannot bypass rest or omit purchased PFP work.
- Explicit physio status uses shared health/physioMapping with self-JWT reads; healed rows get declared regional prehab only, never clear independent injury gates, and active illness moderates down. Why: history notes cannot diagnose or grant clearance.
