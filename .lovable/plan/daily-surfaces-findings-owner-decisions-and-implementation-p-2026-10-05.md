## Two proposals to review before rebuilding

**Build · In-Season · block not set:** This line is the header of `WeeklyRoadmapStrip` in Before You Start. “Build” comes from the athlete-context training-age/competition projection; missing history defaults conservatively to Build. “In-Season” comes from the season resolver, which can be stored, manual or date-based. “Block not set” means the phase is known but its start date is missing or invalid, so the quarter cannot be placed; this is **not** evidence that no workout or training block exists. A conservative quarter volume ceiling is used. The strip already shows a weekly rhythm, today's modalities and a roadmap explainer, but its fallback only points to Profile when the *phase* is unknown, not when the phase is known and the block start is unknown. Proposal: use the same resolved phase/date and training progression lineage displayed in the daily prescription/workout cards; show a plain-language “season dates needed to place your training block” prompt that opens the existing Season Dates dialog when quarterKnown is false; once set, show the actual phase/week from the same source. Keep safety limits and generator unchanged; do not conflate the roadmap rung with the athlete's training-block record. Await owner ruling.

**Human Performance Intelligence:** It appears in the Before You Start drawer and separately on Athlete Command. Today it reads the season phase and a one-time localStorage lifestyle intake (typical sleep vs target, water, stress, constitution, preferred training time). A phase-based formula generates a 0–100 score/band, narrative and phase breath primer; without intake it uses a season baseline. It does **not** read current morning check-in, actual recovery/workload, clip findings, or athlete baselines, and does not feed the daily planner. Proposal: first label season-only/missing-input context honestly rather than presenting it as a live readiness score; then connect to the canonical, consent-appropriate check-in, workload/recovery, analysis-finding and baseline selectors, showing dated evidence and missingness. Let the canonical plan/recovery governor remain authoritative; HPI explains why a prescribed action changed, never creates competing doses or medical claims. Reuse its breath primer where prescribed. Await owner specification before changing scoring or authority.

### Independent morning confirmations — proposed closing order

Current check-in renders one `CheckInLifeChips`/`TellHammersInbox` at the **top**. Its “Nope / Done” currently only sets local state and saves nothing. The next-game choices auto-save a `NOTE` with `payload.kind = next_game_answer` and then hide that section. Proposed final order at the **bottom**: **(1) “Anything change?” with its own Done / Nothing's changed, (2) “When's your next game?” with its own Done / Nothing's changed, (3) Day intent (Rest / Skip / Push), (4) Finish check-in.** These should be visually separate, thumb-sized, and optional. Night check-in's existing entry surface stays intact; Today retains Update Hammer.

Keep the existing entry choices and pain pathway inside “Anything change?”; Done saves the pending entry or confirms that entries already sent are complete, not silently discard typed text. Next-game choices should become selections, not instant sends; its Done saves `next_game_answer`. “Nothing's changed” for each saves its own separate `NOTE` in `schedule_timeline_entries`, tagged with a distinct `payload.kind` and `answer = no_change`; use today's date and read that record back to avoid re-asking on reopen. Preserve all already-saved entries. The existing NOTE merge logic matches kind, text and answer, so repeated confirmations update their respective record without conflating cards. `useScheduleTimeline.save` is the existing writer and refreshes dependent readers. Error states stay visible in each card; a failed save does not clear that card's input and never blocks the check-in's Finish. If the timeline feature is unavailable, do not claim the confirmation was saved; leave check-in Finish available. **Approve the order and voice before changing this behavior.**

### Block-card name and unified source

`WeeklyRoadmapStrip` is the unnamed card. Its `Build` is a training-path rung from athlete context, **not** a block name. `In-Season`/missing block come from `roadmap.quarter` (`resolveSeasonQuarter`) derived from the resolved phase and phase-start date; `useSeasonStatus` has the same season resolver. The approved missing-date link already opens `SeasonDatesDialog`, but the card mixes rung, season phase and weekly template in its header. **Proposed name for owner approval: “Your training block & season.”** Put this heading above the phase and real week, with the training-path rung separately labelled “Your training path: Build” so Build cannot be misread as the block. For a missing phase start, say “Add your season dates to show where you are in your training block” and open the existing dialog; never display “block not set.” For a known phase start, show phase and week from the *same* `roadmap.quarter` resolver used by the daily plan, with `phaseDaysIn` only when it represents that same resolved start date. Workout progression remains read-only from the prescription snapshot; do not invent a week or alter generator/safety limits.
