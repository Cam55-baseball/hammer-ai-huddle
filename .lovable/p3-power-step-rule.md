# P3 Power Step Rule (v3 — supersedes the P3 Do-Not-Cue rule)

**Status:** Active hitting doctrine for baseball and softball. Supersedes
`.lovable/p3-do-not-cue-rule.md`, which is retired and no longer enforced.

## The rule

**P3 is VOLUNTARY.** The stride / power step is a consciously coached,
consciously cued, deliberately trained move. It comes after P1 (back-hip load)
and P2 (hand load).

The hitter strides **at the pitcher's release point** while the pitcher is
working toward release. The target is:

- Front foot **fully down** (whole foot, not just the heel) **at or before
  ball release**
- Landed **sideways**, chest and shoulders square to the plate
- Core tensioned, weight still back, no head or COM drift
- Planted, loaded, and **ready to strike** before the ball is traveling

Order — both what the camera sees and what the hitter feels:
**P1 → P2 → P3 → P4.**

## Coaching language (all approved)

- "Stride to the pitcher's release point."
- "Power step — get the front foot down before he lets it go."
- "Beat the ball with your foot."
- "Land sideways, chest to the plate, weight still back."
- "Start on his move, down at release."

## Graded stride faults (fix them with stride work)

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| Foot down late / after release | Timing reps counting to release; start P1/P2 earlier so the step has room |
| No stride under velocity | Restore the power step; do not let velocity shrink it to nothing |
| Over-stride | Stride-length ceiling reps; land inside the marker |
| Drift (head/weight travels with the step) | Step-and-freeze audits; weight stays back |
| Landing open | Sideways landing audits; outside-third front toss |
| Stiff / locked front leg at landing | Soft-knee landing patterning |

If the stride is chronically late, check that P1 and P2 started early enough —
but the stride itself is a trained, voluntary move, and it is always coached
directly.

## Retired doctrine

The prior "P3 is involuntary / do-not-cue" overlay (and its banned-cue list) is
retired. Athlete-facing surfaces may instruct the stride directly.
`scripts/lint-no-p3-cue.ts` no longer bans stride instruction; it now guards
only against re-introducing do-not-cue / involuntary P3 language.
